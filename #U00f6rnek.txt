1-TOWER CYCLIC RESIN LINE
Compact Resin Treatment Line for Marble and Granite Slabs
The 1 Tower Resin Line is a compact and flexible system for drying, resin application and controlled curing of natural-stone slabs. Designed for factories with moderate production requirements or limited installation space, it combines the essential slab-resining stages around one thermal tower. The oven is configured as a POSITRON cyclic oven.

The line can process one or both surfaces of the slab. For double-sided treatment, the slab is dried, reinforced and cured on the reverse side, turned automatically, and then treated and cured on its visible surface.

The modular structure allows the line to be adapted to marble, granite and other natural stones, including delicate and heavily fissured materials. Additional treatment modules may be incorporated according to the stone characteristics and required automation level.

2-TOWER CYCLIC RESIN LINE
High-Productivity Resin Treatment Line
The 2 Tower Resin Line provides greater thermal capacity and process flexibility by distributing slab drying and resin curing between two oven towers.

For one-side treatment, the two towers allow the drying and curing stages to be managed independently, supporting higher production output and more stable residence times. For double-side treatment, the line carries out drying, reverse-side resin and mesh application, first curing, automatic slab turning, visible-face resin application and final curing within one coordinated production system.
The two-tower architecture is suitable for factories requiring higher output than a compact single-tower line while retaining a relatively efficient factory footprint.

3-TOWER CYCLIC RESIN LINE

The configuration is particularly suitable for factories processing delicate, porous or heavily fissured materials that require reinforcement mesh on the reverse side and a controlled finish on the visible surface.

4-TOWER CYCLIC RESIN LINE
Advanced Resin Treatment Line with Intermediate Honing
The 4 Tower Resin Line is a high-capacity configuration developed for demanding double-sided treatment of natural-stone slabs.

Four thermal towers provide additional flexibility in allocating drying and curing capacity. The architecture incorporates an intermediate honing machine between the reverse-side and visible-face treatment sections. Intermediate honing removes surface irregularities and creates a more uniform upper surface before the visible face is treated. This is particularly valuable for slabs with widespread cracking or uneven surfaces and can improve preparation for the subsequent resin-application stage. Four-tower layouts incorporating a mid-line honing machine are identified in the integrated-line study as an increasingly important architecture for newly established factories.

5-TOWER CYCLIC RESIN LINE
Extended-Curing Resin Line for Direct Polishing Integration
The 5 Tower Resin Line is ION Meccanica’s most advanced resin-treatment architecture.
It adds an additional thermal-curing tower to the four-tower process, increasing the time that treated slabs remain under controlled temperature and airflow conditions before polishing.

The fifth tower is not principally intended to increase hourly throughput beyond the three- or four-tower standard configuration. Its main purpose is to provide extended controlled curing residence time so that epoxy polymerisation can progress further inside the line.

This can reduce or eliminate the conventional requirement to unload slabs after resin treatment, store them externally for continued curing and reload them later onto the polishing line. The additional-tower concept forms the thermal foundation of ION ONEFLOW and enables direct connection between resin treatment and polishing.

1-TOWER STATIC RESIN LINE
Compact Resin Treatment Line with Combined Slab Drying and Resin Curing
The 1-Tower Static Resin Line is a compact natural-stone resin-treatment system designed around one GRAVITON COMBI static oven tower.

GRAVITON COMBI integrates two separate thermal chambers within one insulated vertical structure. One chamber is dedicated to drying slabs before resin application, while the second provides controlled thermal conditions for epoxy resin curing.

The number of drying and curing levels is configured according to the customer’s required production capacity, treatment times and factory layout. Typical arrangements may include 10+20, 15+30 or other project-specific combinations.

The line can be configured for one-side or double-side slab treatment. In a double-side process, the reverse face is dried, resin-treated and reinforced with mesh where required. The slab is then cured, turned automatically and treated on the visible face before completing the final curing stage.

By combining drying and curing within one tower, the system provides controlled static treatment while maintaining a compact factory footprint.

2-TOWER STATIC RESIN LINE
Separated Static Slab Drying and Resin Curing Line
The 2-Tower Static Resin Line provides greater thermal capacity and process flexibility by separating slab drying and resin curing between two static oven towers.

One tower is typically dedicated to drying slabs before resin application, while the second provides the controlled thermal environment required for resin polymerisation. The temperature, airflow and residence time of the two processes can therefore be managed independently.

The system can be built with two standalone GRAVITON towers or as a GRAVITON TANDEM arrangement. In the Tandem configuration, two adjacent oven towers are served by one shared central elevator and tray-transfer system, increasing total capacity while avoiding duplication of the principal handling equipment.

For one-side treatment, slabs move from the drying tower to resin application and then to the curing tower. For double-side treatment, the same line also incorporates reverse-side reinforcement, automatic slab turning, visible-face resin application and the required subsequent curing stages. The number of tray levels in each tower is selected according to the required production output, drying time, curing time and resin-line architecture.

3-TOWER STATIC RESIN LINE
High-Capacity Double-Side Resin Treatment Line
The 3-Tower Static Resin Line is designed for automated double-side resin treatment of marble, granite and other natural-stone slabs.

Three static oven towers allow the principal thermal stages to be separated between slab drying, reverse-side resin curing and final curing after treatment of the visible surface.

A typical process begins with controlled slab drying before resin application. The reverse face is then treated and reinforced with mesh where required before entering the first curing tower. After curing, the slab is turned automatically, treated on its visible face and transferred to the final curing tower.

This separation provides dedicated thermal capacity for each principal stage. It reduces competition between drying and curing operations and allows the required residence time of each process to be managed independently.

The line can be configured with three standalone GRAVITON towers or with one GRAVITON TANDEM system combined with one standalone GRAVITON tower. The 3-Tower Static Resin Line is particularly suitable for porous, fissured or structurally delicate materials requiring controlled reinforcement on both slab surfaces.

Typical Thermal Allocation
•	Tower 1: slab drying before resin application
•	Tower 2: curing after reverse-side resin and mesh application
•	Tower 3: final curing after visible-face resin treatment

The number of tray levels in each tower is selected according to the required process balance and production capacity.

Capacity
The 3-Tower Static Resin Line uses the same nominal production cycle as the standard two-tower configuration.

Double-Side Treatment
•	Production capacity: up to 24 slabs/hour

The principal advantage of the third tower is greater process separation, thermal capacity and production stability rather than a higher nominal hourly output.

Actual capacity depends on the number of tray levels, resin-curing requirements, stone characteristics, slab format and selected automation.
